Clear space and size

R
  • Keep a margin as tall as the "R" on every side.
  • Smallest: horizontal 120 px (30 mm), vertical 72 px (20 mm), mark 20 px (6 mm).
  • Below 32 px use the favicon: it has heavier strokes.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, recolor parts, add effects or retype the name.

App icon and favicons

The coral on the hero's ocean blue with the dotted sea behind it. The maskable version keeps the mark inside Android's safe zone; the Apple icon is square because iOS rounds it.
